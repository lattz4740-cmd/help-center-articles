Ukuvikeleka nobumfihlo lapho usebenzisa Isendlalela

Indlela Isendlalela esivikela ngayo ukuxhumana kwakho ne-inthanethi

Ithrafikhi ye-inthanethi igadwa kakhulu lapho ihamba kunethiwethi yendawo okuyo noma kwekazwelonke.

Isendlalela sisiza ekugcineni ukuxhuma kwakho kuyimfihlo ngokubethela ithrafikhi ye-inthanethi yakho lapho ihamba kunethiwekhi yezwe okulo futhi siyigcina ebitheliwe ize ifike kuseva yeSendlelela. Uma ithrafikhi ibethelwe ngeSendlalela, ababuke inethiwekhi abakwazi ukuhlola amawebhusayithi owavakashele, noma imininingwane oyidlulisile.

Isendlalela singaphinde sikusize ukwazi ukubuyelwa ukufinyelela emathuluzini okuxhumana avikelekile okungenzeka awafinyeleleki ezweni okulo. 

Izindlela zokubethela ezijwayelekile

Isendlalela sibethela ukuxhumana phakathi kwedivayisi yakho neSeva Yesendlalela sisebenzisa icipher eyi-AEAD 256-bit Chacha2020 IETF Poly 1305. Amacipher ayi-AEAD aqinisekisa ubumfihlo, ubuqotho, nobuqiniso, futhi enza ihadiwe yesimanje isebenze ngendlela enhle kakhulu. 

Ukuhlola ukuvikeleka

Ngo-2018, Isendlalela sahlolwa iRadically Open Security neCure53, okuyizinhlangano ezimbili ezizimele zezokuphepha kwedijithali ezibuyekeza isofthiwe ziyiqhathanise nokuvikeleka kwamuva okujwayelekile. IRadically Open Security yenza okunye ukuhlola amabhuku kwango-2022 kwathi iCure53 yahlola i-SDK Yesendlalela ngo-2024. Ungawufunda lapha umbiko:

  • Umbiko Wokuhlola Kokubholayo WeRadically Open Security (Mashi 2018)
  • Umbiko WeCure53 Wokuhlola Kokubholayo Nokuhlola Amabhuku Isendlalela SeJigsaw (Disemba 2018)
  • Umbiko Wokuhlola Kokubholayo WeRadically Open Security (Disemba 2022)
  • Umbiko WeCure53 Wokuhlola Kokubholayo Isendlalela SeJigsaw VPN SDK (Januwari 2024)

Amametric namalogu enziwe angaziwa

Isendlalela sithrekha umkhawulo-kudonsa osetshenzisiwe, "njengamabhayithi adlulisiwe" ngokhiye ngamunye wokufinyelela. Le mininingwane ivumela abalawuli bamaseva ukuba balungise imikhawulo-kudonsa yababhalise kubo ukuze ivumelane neyabahlinzeki beseva yabo yecloud uma kudingeka, kodwa ayibavumeli ukuba babone imininigwane engokoqobo edlule kuseva yeSendlalela.

Funda kabanzi ngokuqoqwa kwedatha nemininingwane yeSendlalela.

Imibuzo Evamile yokuvikeleka nobumfihlo 

Isendlalela singakwazi yini ukungenza ngingaziwa ku-inthanethi?

Cha, Isendlalela asilona ithuluzi elingakwenza ungaziwa. Isendlalela sivikela ubumfihlo bakho kubantu abangase babuke inethiwekhi.

Isendlalela asikwazi ukukwenza ungaziwa ngokuphelele kumawebhusayithi owavakashelayo, ngoba asengakwazi ukukubona lapho ungena ngemvume futhi ngezinye izikhathi esebenzisa amaqhinga athile, njengesigxivizo somunwe sebhrawuza. Kuma-app eselula, amasmathifoni amaningi esimanje anama-API avumela ama-app afakiwe ukuba aveze indawo okuyo engahlangene nommeleli wakho njengoba encike ku-GPS eshumekiwe.

Ngokuvamile ama-VPN ahlinzeka ngezivikelo ezibalulekile, ikakhulukazi ukugadwa ku-inthanethi, nokho zihlezi zikhona izingozi lapho kusetshenziswa i-inthanethi. Ngisho noma une-VPN, uma i-ISP isikwazi kakade ukuthi ungubani futhi iyibona ithrafikhi yakho yethiwekhi, ingakwazi ukuthola ikheli le-IP yeSendlalela seseva yakho. Le mininingwane ingasetshenziselwa ukuvimba ukufinyelela kuseva yeSendlalela noma ukufunda amaphethini endlela osebenza ngayo, njengalapho uvame ukusebenzisa khona i-inthanethi, futhi mhlawumbe nendawo okuyo. 

Abantu bayakwazi yini ukubona ukuthi ngisebenzisa Isendlalela?

Kungenzeka. Izinkundla namasevisi okwazi ukuwafinyelela cishe ayobona ukuthi indlela oxhume ngayo ivela kuseva yecloud. Ngezikhathi ezithile, ayoqaphela ukuthi usebenzisa i-VPN, kodwa ngeke akwazi ukubona okuqukethwe ithrafikhi ye-inthanethi yakho. 

Isendlalela siyakwazi yini ukungivikela kuzo zonke izingozi ezihlobene necyber?

Cha. Alikho ithuluzi elilodwa elingakuvikela kuzo zonke izingozi ezihlobene necyber. Isendlalela sikuvumela ukuba ufinyelele ku-inthanethi evulekile futhi sandisa ubumfihlo bakho ngokubethela ithrafikhi yakho, kodwa esikuncomayo ukuba uthathe izinyathelo ezengeziwe zokuzivikela kwezinye izinhlobo zokuhlaselwa, njengohlelo olungayilungele ikhophyutha nobugebengu bokweba imininingwane ebucayi.

Ukuze uqinise izivikelo zakho lapho usebenzisa i-inthanethi, sicela ucabangele ukusebenzisana nongoti wecybersecurity enhlanganweni yakho. Kungenjalo, ungathola isiqondiso esiqondene nawe siqu kongoti abahamba phambili kwezokuvikeleka kuSecurity Planner, okuyiwebhusayithi eyakhelwe ukukuhlinzeka ngemiyalelo yokukhetha amathuluzi afanele ecybersecurity ngokukukhathazayo.

Ungaphinde uhlole eminye imikhiqizo yecybersecurity kuJigsaw, njenge-Intra, iProject Shield, kanye nePhasiwedi Alert. 

Kusemthethweni yini ukusebenzisa i-VPN?

Sicela ubheke imithetho, imithetho-nqubo, neMigomo Yesevisi yomhlinzeki wecloud ohlela ukuyisebenzisa endaweni ohlala kuyo ngaphambi kokusebenzisa Isendlalela noma ukusebenzisa i-app.

Ngiyisetha kanjani imikhawulo yedatha kokhiye bokufinyelela?

GKMS WEB

Ungasetha umkhawulo wedatha ozosebenza kokhiye bokufinyelela konke. Ukuze usethe umkhawulo, vula Umphathi Wesendlalelo uphinde ufune Kumasethingi. Lapho uzobona ukuthi imikhawulo Yedatha iyakuguqula lokho, uma isetshenzisiwe, ikuvumela ukuthi usethe umkhawulo.

Uma ususethe umkhawulo, ungabona ukuthi umsebenzisi ngamunye useduze kangakanani nomkhawulo ekhasini lokhiye wokufinyelela, lapho igrafu yebha ibonisa khona ukusetshenziswa kwedatha ezinsukwini zokugcina ezingama-30.

Ngaphezu kokwazi ukusetha umkhawulo wabo bonke okhiye bakho bokufinyelela, unganikeza ukhiye ngamunye umkhawulo wawo wedatha. Leli sethingi lizokhipha noma imuphi umkhawulo ozenzakalelayo wedatha owusethile, kepha uma ungazange usethe umkhawulo wedatha ozenzakalelayo, usengasetha umkhawulo wedatha wanoma imuphi ukhiye. 

Ukuze usethe umkhawulo wokhiye wokudlulisela idatha, vula Umphathi Wesendlalelo, funa ngokuya kuthebhu Yokuxhumana efaka ukhiye ofuna ukuwusetha, uphinde uchofoze kumenyu ekuhlangothi olungakwesokudla komcibisholo wokhiye. Kusukela lapho, chofoza Kumkhawulo Wedatha. Ukuze ushintshe umkhawulo wedatha "Kukhiye wami wokufinyelela", chofoza isithonjana Semikhawulo Yedatha .

Khetha okuthi Setha umkhawulo wedatha yomuntu ngamunye. Uma usukhethe leli bhokisi likaqhwi, inkambu izovela lapho ungasetha khona umkhawulo wedatha yomuntu ngamunye kulowo khiye. Chofoza inkinobho yokuthi LONDOLOZA uma usuqedile ukulondoloza umkhawulo wedatha.

Uma usulondoloze umkhawulo wakho wokudlulisela idatha kukhiye okhethiwe, umkhawulo uzoboniswa kusikrini esikhulu, ngokuhambisana nokusetshenziswa kwedatha (ngaphezu kwezinsuku ezingama-30 ezedlule) kukhiye ngamunye.

Ukuze ususe umkhawulo wedatha kusukela kukhiye wokufinyeleleka, funa ngokuya kudayalogi yokhiye Yomkhawulo Wedatha njengaphambilini, susa ukumaka ibhokisi elinelebula ethi Setha umkhawulo wedatha yokomuntu ngamunye, uphinde uchofoze inkinobho ethi LONDOLOZA.

Imibuzo Evamile Yomkhawulo Wedatha

Uyini umkhawulo wedatha olandelayo ngemuva wezinsuku ezingama-30?

Umkhawulo wedatha olandelayo wezinsuku ezingama-30 uzofingqa ukusetshenziswa kokhiye ngamunye ezinsukwini ezingama-30 ezedlule uphinde ugcine ukusetshenziswa kokhiye kuleso sikhathi ngaphansi komkhawulo. Umthelela ukuthi ukhiye awukwazi ukudlula umkhawulo esikhathini sanoma iziphi izinsuku ezingama-30, okufaka izinyanga zekhalenda zezinsuku ezingama-30 noma ngaphansi. Empeleni, lokhu kusho ukuthi idatha etholakalayo yomsebenzisi ngamunye izokhuphuka njalo ngosuku ngenani abalisebenzise ezinsukwini ezingama-31 ezedlule.

Kungani Isendlalelo sisebenzisa imikhawulo elandelayo?

Imikhawulo elandelayo inikeza iziqinisekiso esikhathini esingaphezu kwezinsuku ezingama-30, okusho ukuthi zilungiselelwa kalula kunomkhawulo ophindayo (ofana nosuku olungaqanjwa ngokwabahlinzekelwayo lwenyanga) ngenkathi inikeza iziqinisekiso ngokusemthethweni ezifanayo. Iphinda ifane nokuboniswa okukhona kokusetshenziswa kwedatha Yesendlalelo, kanye namathuluzi ajwayelekile afana namasevisi okuhlaziya nezibalo zeseva.

Iyiphi idatha ebalwa emkhawulweni wedatha?

Ukhiye ngamunye wokufinyelela ophuma kuseva ufakwa esibalweni. Ngokuqondile lokhu kusho idatha ethunyelelwe ukhiye kusukela kuseva, nebuyiselwa kuklayenti. Empeleni, lokhu kufanele kuhambisane eduze nethrafikhi ethunyelwe kusukela kukhiye ukuya kuseva nangemuva, ngakho sethemba ukuthi izofana nezibalo zabasebenzisi bakho. Sikhethe ukuphuma njengoba lokho kube okukhokhiswa abahlinzeki be-cloud esiye sabahlola.

Ingabe abasebenzisi bazokwaziswa uma sebedlule kumkhawulo wabo wedatha?

Hhayi okwamanje. Iningi labahlinzeki be-cloud bafaka umkhawulo ofana ne-1TB ngenyanga egcwele, ongasekela abasebenzisi abayi-10 ngo-100GB noma abasebenzisi abayi-100 ngo-10 GB. Lezi izinombolo ezinkulu kakhulu, futhi asilindele ukuthi abasebenzisi abaningi bazofinyelela kuzo. Sithemba ukuthi abasebenzisi bazoxhumana nabaphathi beseva uma sebafike kumkhawulo wabo. Kodwa, sizolibonga ulwazi lwakho lokuthi izaziso zingasiza kanjani esimweni sakho sokusetshenziswa, futhi ungaxhumana nathi lapha.

Ingabe abasebenzisi bazokwaziswa uma sebasondele emkhawulweni wabo wedatha?

Inani ledatha entsha yokuthi umsebenzisi usondele emkhawulweni wakhe elizotholwa lizohluka ezinsukwini ezihlukile ngoba lisuselwa ekusetshenzisweni kwabo kwezinsuku ezingama-30 ezedlule. Sicabanga ukuthi isexwayoso kungenzeka kakhulu sidide abasebenzisi kunokuba sibasize. Singayijabulela impendulo yakho kulokhu kuziphatha lapha.

Ngingakwazi yini ukusetha kabusha ukusetshenziswa kwedatha yomsebenzisi?

Cha, umkhawulo womsebenzisi uhlala ufake ukusetshenziswa kwedatha kwezinsuku ezingama-30 ezedlule. Kodwa, ungaphakamisa umkhawulo wedatha yabo engukhiye noma ubasungulele ukhiye omusha.

Kungani abanye abasebenzisi bami baye balahlekelwa ukufinyelela ngemuva nje kokuthi ngisebenzise imikhawulo yedatha?

Imikhawulo yedatha isuselwa ezinsukwini ezingama-30 zangaphambili zabasebenzisi zokudlulisela idatha, eqoshwa noma kusetshenziswe noma ingasetshenzisiwe imikhawulo yedatha. Kungenzeka ukuthi abasebenzisi okukhulunywa nabo sebavele badlule kumkhawulo ngaphambi kokuthi ubekwe.  Futhi yazi ukuthi yonke imikhawulo yedatha iyaphoqelelwa, nanoma ushintsha umkhawulo wedatha wokhiye owodwa.

Ngingakwazi yini ukusetha umkhawulo obanzi kuseva, ofana “ne-1 TB njalo ezinsukwini ezingama-30”?

Hhayi okwamanje. Singathanda ukuzwa okuningi mayelana nesimo sakho sokusetshenziswa lapha.

Uma kukhona umkhawulo wedatha ozenzakalelayo kanye nomkhawulo wedatha kukhiye othile, iyiphi ezophoqelelwa?

Umkhawulo wedatha ethile yokhiye uzokhipha noma imuphi umkhawulo wedatha ozenzakalelayo (uma ukhona) owusethile.

Ngingakwazi yini ukusetha umkhawulo wedatha wokhiye othile ngaphandle kokuba nomkhawulo wedatha ozenzakalelayo osethiwe?

Yebo.  Awudingi umkhawulo ozenzakalelayo ochaziwe ukuze usethe umkhawulo wedatha kukhiye owodwa.  Isibonelo, ungasetha umkhawulo kukhiye owodwa ocabanga ukuthi uzokwabiwa ngokusabalala ukuze uzivikele kusukela ekudluliseni idatha okweqile ngalowo khiye.

I-Outline ayifani namasevisi e-VPN othenga ukufinyelela kuwo; iyithuluzi elikuvumela ukuthi usebenzise futhi wabelane ngokufinyelela kuseva ye-VPN egodliwe. Umphathi wesevisi nguye kuphela umuntu onganikeza ukhiye wokufinyelela. Ithimba le-Outline alibasabalalisi okhiye bokufinyelela bamahhala noma abakhokhelwayo. Asinabo ozakwethu bokhiye bokufinyelela be-Outline, ngaphandle kwePaskoocheh ngesiFarsi.

Nakhu okungakhethwa kukho okuthathu kokuthola ukhiye wokufinyelela: 

  • Cela othile ukuthi akumemele kuseva yakhe: Uma wazi othile oneseva ye-Outline, xhumana naye futhi umcele ukhiye wokufinyelela.
  • Sungula iseva ngokwakho: Usebenzisa ikhompyutha yeWindows, yeMac, noma yeLinux, dawuniloda i-Outline Manager futhi usethe iseva ye-Outline yakho egodliwe. Senze le nqubo yaba lula kunoma ngubani ukuba ayenze, ikakhulukazi ngomhlinzeki wesevisi ye-cloud iDigitalOcean.
  • Sebenzisa isevisi ye-Outline noma yeShadowsocks: I-Outline Client ingasetshenziswa namasevisi ezinkampani zangaphandle asebenzisa iphrothokholi ye-Outline noma yeShadowsocks. Ngokwesibonelo:
    • InthLink isebenzisa isevisi yamahhala esekelwe ku-Outline, enohlobo olulungisiwe lweklayenti ye-Outline.
    • I-ASL19 isebenzisa uhlobo oluguquliwe lwe-Outline olubizwa ngeBeePass kanye ne-bot yeTelegram enikeza okhiye bokufinyelela mahhala kumaseva eBeePass.
    • Abasebenzisi bolimi lwesiFarsi bangaphinda bacele ukhiye wokufinyelela we-Outline ku-ASL19’s Paskoocheh.

Ngemva kokudawuniloda i-app ye-Outline Client, kopisha futhi unamathisele ukhiye wakho wokufinyelela ku-Outline Client ukuze uxhume kuseva ye-Outline.
