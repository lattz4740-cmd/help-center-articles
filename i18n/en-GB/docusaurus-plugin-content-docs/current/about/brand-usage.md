---
title: "Am I allowed to use or replicate the Outline brand?"
sidebar_label: "Am I allowed to use or replicate the Outline brand?"
---

While Outline is open source and free to use, our brand is proprietary to Jigsaw and should not be misused. Please follow the [user guide](https://github.com/Jigsaw-Code/outline-brand/blob/main/outline_brand_user_guide.pdf) to ensure that you don't make potentially misleading claims or mischaracterise your relationship with Outline or with Jigsaw, and are correctly using the approved branding elements.
