title: "Do you support 'getoutline.me' and the '@OutlineVpnOfficial' Telegram channel?"
sidebar_label: "Do you support 'getoutline.me' and the '@OutlineVpnOfficial' Telegram channel?"
---

The Outline team has received numerous enquiries about the website '[getoutline.me](http://getoutline.me/)' and its associated Telegram channel '@OutlineVpnOfficial'. These services are not affiliated with the Outline team and we cannot assist users experiencing issues with access keys from these services.
