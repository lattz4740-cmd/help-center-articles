Thanks for helping make Outline better! There are a few ways to send us feedback, either anonymously or with your email if you require a response:

- **For developers**: Outline is open source software and we host all our code on [GitHub](https://github.com/jigsaw-Code/?q=outline). You can share your ideas on GitHub or on our [Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/outlinevpn/) community.
- **For users**: You can send us feedback through the Outline or Outline Manager apps. Tell us what your feedback is about and, optionally, leave an email so we can write you back about it. We also collect some metadata about your environment, so that we can help you with troubleshooting, like your browser, operating system, and which software version you’re on.
- **Through this site**: You can click on the contact us link anywhere you see it within this site.
- **Serious issues**: If you want to report a serious security issue, we’d appreciate you email us with details at security [at] getoutline.org.

Thank you again for taking the time to share your thoughts with us.
