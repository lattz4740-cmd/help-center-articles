---
title: "Can I use an access key more than once?"
sidebar_label: "Can I use an access key more than once?"
---

Yes. You can use your [access key](/about/terminology) on any number of your devices, but we do not recommend that you share your key with another person. Someone with your access key could potentially inspect all your Internet traffic if they have access to it, which no longer makes your Internet usage secure or private.
