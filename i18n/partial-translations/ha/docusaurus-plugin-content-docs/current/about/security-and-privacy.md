Tsaro da sirri yayin amfani da Outline

## Yadda Outline ke ba wa sadarwarka ta intanet kariya

Zirga-zirgar intanet na cikin haɗarin leƙen asiri yayin da take gudana ta hanyar sadarwarka ta cikin gida ko ta ƙasa.

Outline na taimakawa wajen sirranta sadarwarka ta hanyar ɓoye zirga-zirgar intanet ɗinka yayin da take gudana ta hanyar sadarwarka ta ƙasa, sannan ya ci gaba da sakaye ta har sai ta kai sabar Outline. Yayin da aka ɓoye zirga-zirgar intanet da Outline, masu leƙen asiri na hanyar sadarwa ba za su iya duba shafukan da kake ziyarta ko bayanan da kake aikawa ba.

Outline na iya taimaka maka wajen sake samun damar amfani da kayan aikin sadarwa masu kariyar baki-da-baki da ba za a iya samunsu a ƙasarka ba.

## Mizanin ɓoyewa

Outline na ɓoye sadarwa tsakanin na'urarka da Sabar Outline ta amfani da makullan sirri na AEAD 256-bit Chacha2020 IETF Poly 1305. Makullan sirri na AEAD na sirrantawa, daidaitawa da ingantawa, kuma suna yin aiki mai kyau a kan na'urorin zamani.

## Binciken tsaro

A shekarar 2018, Radically Open Security da Cure53, kamfanonin ba da tsaro na dijital masu zaman kansu da ke tantance manhaja bisa sabbin mizanin tsaro, sun gudanar da bincike kan Outline. Radically Open Security sun sake gudanar da wani binciken a shekarar 2022, yayin da Cure53 suka gudanar da bincike kan Outline SDK a 2024. Za ka iya karanta rahoton a nan:

- [Rahoton Gwajin Tsaron Radically Open Security (Maris 2018)](https://getoutline.org/reports/ros-report.pdf)
- [Rahoton Gwajin Tsaro da Bincike na Cure53 Jigsaw Outline (Disamba 2018)](https://getoutline.org/reports/cure53-report.pdf)
- [Rahoton Gwajin Tsaro na Radically Open Security (Disamba 2022)](https://getoutline.org/reports/ros-report-2022.pdf)
- [Rahoton Gwajin Tsaro na Cure53 Jigsaw Outline VPN SDK (Janairu 2024)](https://getoutline.org/reports/cure53-report-SDK-2024.pdf)

## Sakayayyun ma'aunai da bayanai

Outline na bin diddigin maƙurar yawan bayanan da aka yi amfani da su, ta hanyar “bytes da aka tura” ga kowane makullin shiga. Wannan bayanai na ba wa masu gudanar da sabar damar daidaita rijistar maƙurar yawan bayanai tare da kamfanoninsu na sabar intanet gwargwadon buƙata, amma ba ya ba su damar ganin ainihin bayanan da suka wuce ta sabar Outline.

Ƙara sani game da [tattara bayanai](/about/data-collection) na Outline.

## Tambayoyin da ake yawan yi na tsaro da tsare sirri

## Shin Outline zai iya sakaya ni a intanet?

A'a, Outline ba kayan aikin sakayewa ba ne. Outline na kare sirrinka daga masu leƙen asiri na hanyar sadarwa.

Outline ba ya sakaye ka gabaɗaya a shafukan yanar gizon da kake ziyarta, saboda har yanzu akan iya gane ka yayin da ka shiga, kuma wani lokaci ta hanyoyi kamar shedar yatsa na burauza. Ga manhajojin waya, yawancin wayoyin zamani suna da API da ke ba da damar manhajojin da aka dasa su gano wurin da kake ba tare da dogara da manhajar sirri ba, saboda suna iya amfani da GPS da ke cikin wayar.

Galibi VPN na ba da muhimmiyar kariya, musamman daga leƙen asiri na intanet, amma duk da haka akwai haɗarin amfani da intanet. Ko da kana amfani da VPN, idan kamfanin sadarwa ya riga ya san shaidarka kuma yana iya lura da zirga-zirgarka, yana iya gano adireshin IP na sabarka ta Outline. Za a iya amfani da wannan bayanan don toshe damar shiga sabar Outline ko gano tsarin amfani, kamar lokutan da ka fi hawa intanet, da kuma yiwuwar gano wurin da kake.

## Shin wani zai san ina amfani da Outline?

Me yiwuwa ne. Dandaloli da manhajojin da kake amfani da su za su iya gane cewa haɗinka yana zuwa ne daga sabar intanet. A wasu lokuta, suna iya gano cewa kana amfani da VPN, amma ba za su iya ganin abubuwan da ke cikin zirga-zirgar intanet ɗinka ba.

## Shin Outline zai kare ni daga duk wata barazanar yanar gizo?

A'a. Babu wani kayan aiki guda ɗaya da zai iya kare ka daga duk wata barazanar yanar gizo. Outline zai ba ka damar shiga buɗaɗɗen intanet kuma ya ƙara sirrinka ta hanyar ɓoye zirga-zirgarka, amma muna ba da shawarar ɗaukar ƙarin matakai don kare kanka daga sauran nau'ukan hare-hare kamar malwaya da satar bayanai ta intanet.

Don ƙarfafa matakan tsaron intanet ɗinka, muna ba da shawarar ka tuntuɓi ƙwararre kan tsarn intanet na kamfaninka. Haka nan, za ka iya samun shawarwari na musamman daga manyan masana harkar tsaro a [Security Planner](https://securityplanner.org/), wani shafin yanar gizo da aka samar don ba ka cikakken jagoranci kan yadda za ka zaɓi kayan aikin tsaron intanet da suka dace da buƙatunka.

Kuma za ka iya duba sauran kayan aikin tsaron intanet daga [Jigsaw](https://jigsaw.google.com/), kamar [Intra](https://getintra.org/), [Project Shield](https://g.co/shield), da [Password Alert](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/password-alert/noondiphcddnnabmjcihcjfbhfklnnep?).

## An amince a yi amfani da VPN?

Daure ka bincika dokokin gida, ƙa'idoji, da Sharuɗɗan Amfani na kamfanin intanet da kake shirin amfani da shi kafin ka fara aiki da Outline ko amfani da manhajar.
