GKMS ARTICLE TITLE
Why is Outline harder to block than a most other VPNs?

GKMS WEB

Outline is tricky for blockers because:

  1. It uses a special code that's hard to recognize, unlike other VPNs with easy-to-spot codes.
  2. Its list of servers is kept private, so it's not easy for blockers to find.
  3. Even if a blocker manages to stop one of Outline's servers, it can quickly create a new one.
