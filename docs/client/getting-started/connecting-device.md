---
title: Connecting your device to an Outline server
sidebar_label: Connecting your device
---

In order to connect your device to an Outline server, a server administrator has to send you an invitation to connect to their server with a unique [access key](/about/terminology). This invitation will:

- Prompt you to download and install Outline on your device if you don't already have it
- Use the access key to make a secure connection between you and the Outline server

Once you have established your connection to the server, you can click "connect" or "disconnect" to use Outline.

:::warning[Important]
Outline does not work without an access key and an Outline server. Downloading and installing the Outline app without a key won't protect your internet traffic.
:::
