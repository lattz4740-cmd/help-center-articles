---
title: "Am I allowed to use or replicate the Outline brand?"
sidebar_label: Brand usage
---

While Outline is open source and free to use, our brand is proprietary to Jigsaw and should not be misused. Please follow the [user guide](https://github.com/Outline-Foundation/outline-brand/blob/main/outline_brand_user_guide.pdf) to ensure you do not make potentially misleading claims or mischaracterize your relationship with Outline or with Jigsaw, and are correctly using the approved branding elements.
